About

I'm a Research Scientist at Mistral AI, where I lead the Multimodal team.

Our team works across both pre-training and post-training, tackling a range of research questions, such as science of data, robotics and multimodal agents. If this sounds interesting to you — reach out!

Previously, I completed a PhD with the Visual Geometry Group (VGG) at the University of Oxford, working on representation learning in computer vision, where I was fortunate to be supervised by Andrew Zisserman and Andrea Vedaldi. During my PhD, I also spent time at Meta AI: first with Ishan Misra in New York, and then in the Segment Anything team with Ross Girshick.
